The Challenge We Solve

Traditional SCADA/HMI systems optimize for visualization and static thresholds, missing subtle early warning signs that lead to costly failures.

The Problem

  • • Issues begin as weak signals that never cross hard thresholds
  • • Operators face late alerts, noisy alarms, and limited context
  • • Delayed response leads to unplanned stoppages and waste
  • • No time-context modeling or behavioral pattern detection

Our Solution

  • • Real-time analytics detect small deviations and patterns early
  • • Context-rich alerts via WhatsApp/Telegram with dashboard links
  • • Lightweight layer on top of existing PLC infrastructure
  • • Historical trend analysis for root-cause investigation
